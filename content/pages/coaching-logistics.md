## Availability

I meet with clients for 60 minute sessions via phone or Zoom on Mon-Thu at 11am, 1pm, or 3pm ET. I occasionally take calls outside those hours, but don't expect it! I am not available on Fridays. You will work with my assistant to schedule a regular slot or reschedule in the event something comes up last minute.

## Cadence

I meet with most clients every other week, but it's up to you what frequency is right. I work with some people once a week and others once a month. I ask clients to book a regular slot so we can balance busy calendars.

## Cancellations & Rescheduling

A big part of having a regularly scheduled session is so you can plan for it. In the event that something comes up last-minute, my assistant will do her best to reschedule you for the same week or the week after. Cancellations and unsuccessful reschedules done within 7 days of the originally scheduled session will be billed at my regular rate. Conversely, if I have to cancel a session for any reason, that's on me and I'll adjust your rate for that month on a pro-rated basis. See more detail on my scheduling, rescheduling, cancellation, and termination policies [here](https://andy-sparks.notion.site/Scheduling-Rescheduling-Cancellation-and-Refund-Policies-5a17092b09c44b83a3b9f27736a66bba).

## Fees

My rates depend on a company's stage, but I tend to price on the high-end relative to the executive coaching market ([Pave, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/matt-schulman-15911861_ceo-executive-coach-compensation-benchmarks-activity-7024830363961098240-UUQk)). My rates start at $2,000 / session or $4,000 / mo. for sessions every other week and will be adjusted in the event you'd like to meet less often or more often.

## Billing

When you sign your contract, I'll collect the information I need to send you a bill. I accept payment via ACH and invoices go out between the 1st and 5th of the month. My assistant will handle any adjustments we need to make in the event of any cancellations or rescheduling on your or my side.
